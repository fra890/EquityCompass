Compliance summary: "Equity Compensation Planning" film (40 s, LinkedIn, three crops)

This is an advertisement under the Marketing Rule: a 40-second silent-first video intended for Frank Alvarez's LinkedIn profile. It contains no performance, returns, share prices, price views, testimonials, endorsements, awards, rankings, media logos, client names or client details, and no people or voiceover. Every statement on screen is listed here. (1) Hook: "If you work at SpaceX, your next vest has a tax gap." The employer appears as plain text only, with no logo, product or trade dress; the build can substitute "Anduril" or "Tesla". The statement is categorical and is true only for a viewer whose marginal federal rate exceeds 22%; a softer "may have" version is available. (2) Example labels: "RSU vest: $100,000 (example)", "Withheld: $22,000", "Still owed at a 37% federal bracket: $15,000". Arithmetic: $100,000 x 22% = $22,000; $100,000 x 37% = $37,000; $37,000 - $22,000 = $15,000. The figures are federal income tax only, treat the full vest as taxed at 37%, and leave out state and payroll taxes. (3) Caption: "22% is withheld. Your bracket may be higher." This refers to the flat federal supplemental withholding rate; advisor to substantiate that 22% is the rate applied to the audience's vests. (4) Scene labels: "Lockup release / example date" (twice), "ISO lot", "1 year from exercise", "2 years from grant" (the ISO qualifying-disposition holding periods), and "AMT" on a gauge with no scale that rises when a lot is exercised. Caption: "Lockups. ISOs. AMT. Each one has a date." No dates, amounts or prices are shown. (5) Sequence labels: "1 Vest, 2 Set aside tax, 3 Exercise, 4 Sell, 5 Diversify", with the caption "The plan is deciding before the date, not after." This is a generic order of decisions, not a recommendation to any person; one tall glass tower becoming several of similar height is a picture of diversification and carries no number or outcome claim. (6) End card: Tidemark logo; "Equity Compensation Planning"; "Frank Alvarez, Financial Consultant | Tidemark Financial Partners"; "Fee-Only · Fiduciary · Independent" (the firm's tagline as written; title as supplied by marketing, to be confirmed). The line "Quoted in Bloomberg, NYT DealBook and Yahoo Finance (2026)" exists in the build but is switched OFF and does not appear. (7) Disclosure on the end card, held about 8 seconds: "Example figures are hypothetical and for illustration only. Tidemark, LLC is an SEC-registered investment adviser. Registration does not imply a certain level of skill or training. For informational purposes only; not investment, tax or legal advice. Investing involves risk, including loss of principal. Disclosures: tidemarkfp.com". The words guaranteed, best, top, maximize, safe and always do not appear. Also for review: the accompanying LinkedIn caption repeats the same example and disclosure, and Frank's personal LinkedIn account needs approval for business use before the film is pinned.

Compliance notes
