If you work at SpaceX, here is how tax on an RSU vest usually works.

When shares vest, federal income tax is typically withheld at a flat 22%. That is a withholding rate, not your tax rate. If your income puts you in a higher bracket, the difference is still owed when you file.

Example: a $100,000 vest. $22,000 is withheld. At a 37% federal bracket, the tax is $37,000. The $15,000 difference is the gap.
