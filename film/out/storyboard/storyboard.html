Equity compensation film: storyboard for approval

A 40-second, faceless, sound-off film for Frank Alvarez's LinkedIn profile. These are low-resolution Blender (Cycles) stills from the actual scene files, with the real captions and end card composited. Nothing below is a mock-up; the full render uses the same code at higher quality.

22.0 – 32.0 s

Beat 4. Deciding before the date

Shot

Five brass marks start out of order on the rail and slide into sequence inside a glass channel. Water (IOR 1.33, real refraction) rises past each engraved mark; each label turns gold as the tide line passes it. Behind, one tall glass tower lowers while six more rise into an even, lit skyline.

Camera

85 mm from slightly above, slow arc left to right. Skyline is seen above the water and refracted through it.

Light

Blue hour. Towers lit warm from inside.

On-screen text

Caption, 22.6 to 32.0 sThe plan is deciding before the date, not after.

Labels in the scene

  • 1 Vest → 2 Set aside tax → 3 Exercise → 4 Sell → 5 Diversify

The skyline is a picture of spreading one holding across several. It carries no number and no claim about results.

For the compliance officer to decide

  1. The hook is categorical. “Your next vest has a tax gap” is true only when the viewer's marginal federal rate is above 22%. The caption that follows says “may be higher”. A softer hook is one word away: “your next vest may have a tax gap.” I kept your wording.
  2. The $15,000 is federal income tax only and treats the whole vest as taxed at 37%. State tax and payroll taxes are left out. The label says “at a 37% federal bracket” and “(example)”; the disclosure says figures are hypothetical.
  3. The film names an employer in text. No logo, product or trade dress appears anywhere.
  4. It will sit on a personal LinkedIn profile. Firm policy needs that account approved for business use.
  5. Title and disclosure are used exactly as briefed, including “Financial Consultant” and “Fee-Only · Fiduciary · Independent”.

Known limits in these stills

  • Stills are rendered at 40–60% size with few samples, so glass and water are softer and noisier than the final.
  • In 16:9 the scene labels are small when the video plays in a phone feed. The captions carry the message there; 16:9 reads best on desktop.
  • Beat 1 holds the hook for the full 3 s; the block is still. Movement starts at 3.0 s.
  • Cuts between beats will be 0.4 s dissolves.
