# ESPP explainer: computed numbers

Generated by `npm run numbers:espp` from `src/espp/model.ts`. Do not edit by hand.
Hypothetical, per share, Section 423 qualified plan. The model splits the gain into
ordinary income and capital gain; it applies no tax rates.

## Inputs (FRANK: VERIFY each one)

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Plan discount | 15% |
| Lookback | yes (lower of offering-date and purchase-date price) |
| Price on the offering date | $20.00 |
| Price on the purchase date | $30.00 |
| Sale price | $50.00 |
| Offering period | 6 months |
| Qualifying holding periods | more than 24 months from the offering date and more than 12 months from the purchase date |
| Example disqualifying sale | month 12 (6 months after purchase) |

## Results (per share)

| Item | How | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price paid | 15% off $20.00 | $17.00 |
| Total gain at sale | sale price - price paid | $33.00 |
| Disqualifying: ordinary income | purchase-date price - price paid | $13.00 |
| Disqualifying: capital gain | sale price - purchase-date price (short-term) | $20.00 |
| Qualifying: ordinary income | lesser of 15% x offering-date price ($3.00) or the gain ($33.00) | $3.00 |
| Qualifying: long-term capital gain | total gain - ordinary income | $30.00 |
| First month both holding periods are met | later of the two clocks | month 24 |

## What the model leaves out

- Tax rates and dollars of tax. Only the character of the gain is shown.
- State tax, NIIT, and payroll taxes.
- Plans without a lookback, with a smaller discount, or with purchase limits (the $25,000 annual limit is not modeled).
- Sales below the price paid in a qualifying disposition (ordinary income is then zero and the loss is a capital loss).
- Non-qualified ESPPs, which are taxed at purchase.
